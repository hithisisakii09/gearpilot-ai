## Registry and safeguards

`src/data/affiliate-programs.json` was not modified. All eight merchants remain `not_applied`, with no approved destination domains, program approval evidence, tracking references, or active links. Branch's CJ application link is not an approval and was not opened as an application workflow.

No commission rate, cookie duration, geographic eligibility, or promotion restriction is asserted except for the merchant's qualitative Branch page wording. Do not infer the contents of inaccessible terms or of a network listing not linked/confirmed by a merchant source.
