# Phase 4.0 website credibility audit

## Findings and changes

- **Home:** describes the genuine US-focused standing-desk and home-office discovery purpose without asserting verified reviews, meaningful traffic, partnerships, or earnings. Synthetic recommendations are clearly identified as a demo.
- **About:** added a concise editorial-purpose page. It does not invent an operator name, address, contact channel, research history, or first-hand testing.
- **Methodology:** explains that candidate research, configuration-specific product evidence, offer eligibility, and affiliate approval are separate. It clarifies that demo content is not verified product advice and that commissions do not affect ordering.
- **Contact:** remains noindex and does not publish an invented email or identity. An owner must supply and monitor a suitable contact channel before an application or commercial launch.
- **Privacy:** retains explicit limitations about operator/contact and hosting data-processing details. This is not a legal-compliance determination; owner and qualified legal review are outstanding.
- **Affiliate disclosure:** states the current no-program/no-live-link/no-earnings status and describes commissions only as a hypothetical future possibility.

## Editorial integrity

The existing standing-desk fit guide was expanded with useful, non-product-specific guidance on room footprint, adjustment-range fit, stability tradeoffs, total cost, return constraints, and checking evidence against the selected configuration. It does not claim first-hand testing, invent product data, or add purchase links or Product/Offer structured data.

## Owner review needed

Supply and review the real operator/legal identity, monitored contact address, applicable privacy and hosting processing/retention details, and jurisdiction-specific legal/disclosure wording before monetized launch. Do not publish placeholders. Current affiliate registry and catalog data were intentionally left unchanged.
