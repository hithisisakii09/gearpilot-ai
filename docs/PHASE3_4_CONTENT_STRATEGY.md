# Phase 3.4 editorial content strategy

## Initial content

Added one curated Astro page at `/guides/standing-desk-fit/`, linked from site navigation, the homepage, and a visible breadcrumb. The page is an original planning checklist, not a product review. It focuses on measuring a workspace and checking exact configuration, adjustment range, stated capacity, warranty, return terms, and evidence.

The text does not state unsupported universal dimensions, medical/health benefits, product claims, current prices, merchant policies, or hands-on testing. It distinguishes user-specific fit from published desk facts and directs readers to the site's evidence methodology. The guide clearly labels the public catalog as synthetic.

## Content model decision

A full content collection is not justified for one tightly controlled guide. Keep this page as a reviewed Astro route; introduce a collection with required source/author/review frontmatter only when a sustained editorial program exists. Do not generate candidate-specific pages from catalog JSON.

## Research and review rules

Specific product/specification claims require a source captured in the existing Phase 3 source register and product provenance. Commercial facts need exact-configuration observations, freshness, and human editorial approval under Phase 3.3. Never represent research candidates or demo records as endorsements, reviews, or real offers.

The official OSHA workstation reference could not be retrieved during this task (HTTP 403 from the documentation fetch tool); no OSHA-derived claim or citation was added. External citations must be checked for relevance and availability before publication.
