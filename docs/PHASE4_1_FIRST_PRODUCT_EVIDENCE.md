## Minimum human collection actions

1. Researcher manually opens the official US manufacturer page in a normal browser without bypassing restrictions.
2. Selects a currently purchasable exact frame, desktop size/material/finish, control/accessory options, and records the displayed SKU or unambiguous variant identifier.
3. Captures manufacturer specification source and the selected configuration’s dimensions, adjustment range (including whether desktop is included), capacity, and applicable warranty/return source.
4. Captures the current cart/checkout summary before purchase only, including USD item price, mandatory shipping/charges, destination ZIP state if needed, stock/delivery status, and timestamp in UTC. Do not purchase.
5. Records evidence URLs and exact capture time; redact personal, payment, address, account, and order data.
6. Uses their real name as collector in the existing evidence workflow; a different authorized human independently reviews the evidence. Never create an observation from this report alone.
7. Leave all unavailable or ambiguous values `UNKNOWN`. Only after approval should product verification and offer eligibility be evaluated separately.

No product or offer was promoted, verified, or published in Phase 4.1.
